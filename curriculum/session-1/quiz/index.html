Readings for the week

Key question: Why does my heart long for "something more", and who is calling me?

From the Catechism

CCC 1–3, 27–30, 31–35, 44–45, 1229

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 1–3
  • CCC 27–30
  • CCC 31–35
  • CCC 44–45
  • CCC 1229, 1233
  • CCC 1271–1272

From the Bible

  • Acts 17:22–28. Paul in Athens speaks of the "unknown God" who "is not far from each one of us" (CCC 28 quotes this passage).
  • Psalm 42:1–2. The soul thirsts for God like a deer for running streams.
  • John 1:35–39. Jesus asks the first disciples, "What are you looking for?" and invites them: "Come and see."

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice According to the Catechism, why did God create us?

    1. A. Because he needed servants
    2. B. To share his own blessed life
    3. C. Because he was lonely
    4. D. To test whether we would fail

  2. True or false The Catechism calls the human person "a religious being".

    Circle one: True / False

  3. Multiple choice Which psalm says the soul thirsts for God like a deer for running streams?

    1. A. Psalm 23
    2. B. Psalm 42
    3. C. Psalm 95
    4. D. Psalm 130

  4. Multiple choice In John 1:35–39, what does Jesus ask the first disciples?

    1. A. Why are you afraid?
    2. B. What are you looking for?
    3. C. Where are you from?
    4. D. Who sent you?

  5. Short answer Name two "ways to God": things that point us to God.

  6. True or false Reason can know that God exists, but Revelation tells us who he is.

    Circle one: True / False

  7. Short answer In Athens, Paul speaks of the "unknown God" who "is not far from each one of us". Which book of the Bible tells this story?

  8. Multiple choice From the time of the apostles, how have people become Christians?

    1. A. All at once, in a single day
    2. B. In stages: hearing the Word, conversion, profession of faith, Baptism, the gift of the Spirit and the Eucharist
    3. C. Only by being born into a Christian family
    4. D. By a private decision, without the Church

  9. True or false People never ignore or reject God.

    Circle one: True / False

Your catechist has the answers.
