Mary, Mother of God and Our Mother

Readings for the week

Key question: Why do Catholics honour Mary, and is it worship?

From the Catechism

CCC 484–507, 963–972, 2673–2679

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 484–507
  • CCC 726
  • CCC 963–972
  • CCC 2673–2679

From the Bible

  • Luke 1:26–38. The Annunciation.
  • Luke 1:39–56. The Visitation and the Magnificat: "All generations will call me blessed."
  • John 2:1–11. Cana: "Do whatever he tells you."
  • John 19:25–27. "Behold, your mother."
