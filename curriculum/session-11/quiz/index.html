Session 11 · Readings and quiz

Ancestors, the Communion of Saints and Prayer for the Dead

Back to Session 11 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: How does a Christian honour the ancestors faithfully?

From the Catechism

CCC 843, 946–962, 1032, 1371, 1674–1676, 2110–2117, 2299–2301

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 828, 843
  • CCC 946–962
  • CCC 1032
  • CCC 1371
  • CCC 1670, 1674–1676
  • CCC 2110–2117
  • CCC 2299–2301

From the Bible

  • Hebrews 12:1–2. "So great a cloud of witnesses."
  • 2 Maccabees 12:43–46. Judas Maccabeus offers a sacrifice for the dead (quoted at CCC 1032).
  • Luke 20:37–38. God is "not God of the dead, but of the living; for all live to him."
  • Deuteronomy 18:10–12. Divination, sorcery and consulting the dead are forbidden.
  • Exodus 20:12. Honour your father and your mother.

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice What are the three states of the Church?

    1. A. Pilgrims on earth, those being purified after death, and those in glory
    2. B. Priests, religious and lay people
    3. C. Africa, Europe and the Americas
    4. D. Past, present and future

  2. True or false Death interrupts the union between the living and those who have died in Christ.

    Circle one: True / False

  3. Short answer Which Old Testament book tells of Judas Maccabeus offering a sacrifice for the dead?

  4. Multiple choice Above all, what does the Church offer for the dead?

    1. A. Money
    2. B. The Eucharist
    3. C. Animal sacrifices
    4. D. Charms

  5. True or false Burying the dead is a corporal work of mercy.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Multiple choice According to the first commandment, the honour given to God alone:

    1. A. May be shared with ancestors and spirits
    2. B. Must not be given to any creature, including ancestors and spirits
    3. C. Is only for Sundays
    4. D. Applies only to priests

  7. Short answer Name two practices the Catechism says are contrary to the honour due to God.

  8. Multiple choice In Luke 20:37–38, Jesus says God is not God of the dead but of:

    1. A. The angels
    2. B. The living
    3. C. The priests
    4. D. Israel only

  9. True or false Popular piety never needs to be purified or corrected.

    Circle one: True / False
