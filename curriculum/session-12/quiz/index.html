Readings for the week

Key question: What happens when we die, and what do we hope for?

From the Catechism

CCC 988–1004, 1005–1014, 1020–1029, 1030–1032, 1033–1037, 1038–1041, 1042–1050

From the Bible

  • John 11:25–26. "I am the resurrection and the life."
  • 1 Corinthians 15:35–58. The resurrection body; "O death, where is your victory?"
  • 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18. Do not grieve "as others do who have no hope."
  • Matthew 25:31–46. "As you did it to one of the least of these my brethren, you did it to me."
  • Revelation 21:1–5. "He will wipe away every tear from their eyes."

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. True or false We believe that on the last day our mortal bodies will be raised and reunited with our souls.

    Circle one: True / False

  2. Multiple choice What does the Church teach about reincarnation?

    1. A. We live many lives
    2. B. We live only once: there is no reincarnation
    3. C. Only good people return
    4. D. The Church has no teaching

  3. Multiple choice When does the particular judgement take place?

    1. A. At death
    2. B. At Baptism
    3. C. At the end of each year
    4. D. Never

  4. Short answer Describe heaven in one sentence.

  5. Multiple choice Who is in purgatory?

    1. A. Those who rejected God
    2. B. Those who die in God's grace and friendship but are still imperfectly purified
    3. C. The angels
    4. D. People waiting to be reborn

  6. True or false God predestines some people to hell.

    Circle one: True / False

  7. Short answer How does the Catechism describe hell?

  8. Multiple choice Who says, "I am the resurrection and the life"?

    1. A. Moses
    2. B. Jesus
    3. C. St Paul
    4. D. St Peter

  9. Short answer In Matthew 25:31–46, what does Christ say about what we did to "one of the least of these my brethren"?
