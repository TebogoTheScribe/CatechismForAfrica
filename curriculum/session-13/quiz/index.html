Liturgy, Sunday and the Liturgical Year

Back to Session 13 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: Why do Catholics worship the way they do, and why is Sunday so important?

From the Catechism

CCC 1066–1068, 1136–1144, 1145–1162, 1163–1173, 1204–1206, 2174–2188

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 1066–1068
  • CCC 1136–1144
  • CCC 1145–1162

  • CCC 1204–1206
  • CCC 1345

From the Bible

  • Acts 2:42–47. The apostles' teaching, fellowship, the breaking of bread and the prayers.
  • Luke 24:1, 13–35. On "the first day of the week", the risen Christ is known in the breaking of bread.
  • Psalm 118:24. "This is the day which the Lord has made."
  • Revelation 1:10. "I was in the Spirit on the Lord's day."

    1. A. Christ's Paschal Mystery
    2. B. Only the birth of Jesus
    3. C. The parish's history
    4. D. National holidays

    1. A. Christmas
    2. B. Easter, the "Feast of feasts"
    3. C. Pentecost
    4. D. All Saints

    2. B. It is the day of creation only
