Baptism: Born of Water and the Spirit

Back to Session 14 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: What does Baptism actually do, and why can it be received only once?

From the Catechism

CCC 1213–1216, 1217–1228, 1229–1233, 1234–1245, 1253–1255, 1256, 1257–1261, 1262–1274

From the Bible

  • John 3:1–8. "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit…"
  • Romans 6:3–11. Buried with Christ, raised to new life.
  • Matthew 28:18–20. The great commission.
  • Acts 2:38. "Repent, and be baptised… and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Spirit."
  • Ezekiel 36:25–27. "I will sprinkle clean water upon you… a new heart I will give you."
  • Galatians 3:27. "As many of you as were baptised into Christ have put on Christ."

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice What does the Catechism call Baptism?

    1. A. An optional blessing
    2. B. "The gateway to life in the Spirit" and "the door which gives access to the other sacraments"
    3. C. A family custom
    4. D. A sign of adulthood

  2. Short answer Name one Old Testament event that points to Baptism.

  3. Multiple choice In necessity, who may baptise?

    1. A. Only a bishop
    2. B. Anyone with the right intention, using water and the Trinitarian formula
    3. C. Nobody
    4. D. Only a godparent

  4. True or false Baptism can be repeated if someone falls away from the faith.

    Circle one: True / False

  5. Multiple choice Which sins does Baptism forgive?

    1. A. Only original sin
    2. B. All sins, original and personal, with all punishment due
    3. C. Only small sins
    4. D. None

  6. True or false God is bound by his sacraments, so no one at all can be saved without water Baptism.

    Circle one: True / False

  7. Short answer In John 3:1–8, Jesus tells Nicodemus that one must be born of what?

  8. Short answer Complete Galatians 3:27: "As many of you as were baptised into Christ have ..."

  9. Short answer How do godparents help?
