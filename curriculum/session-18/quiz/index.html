Healing Sacraments: Reconciliation and Anointing of the Sick

Back to Session 18 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: How does Christ forgive and heal us through his Church?

CCC 1420–1429, 1440–1449, 1450–1460, 1461–1467, 1468–1470, 1499–1513, 1514–1525

From the Bible

  • Luke 15:11–32. The prodigal son and the merciful father (CCC 1439).
  • John 20:19–23. "Receive the Holy Spirit. If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven."
  • James 5:14–16. Anointing the sick; "confess your sins to one another."
  • Mark 2:1–12. Jesus forgives and heals the paralysed man.

Quiz

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice What are the two sacraments of healing?

    1. A. Baptism and Confirmation
    2. B. Reconciliation (Penance) and the Anointing of the Sick
    3. C. Marriage and Holy Orders
    4. D. Eucharist and Baptism

  2. True or false Only God forgives sins.

    Circle one: True / False

  3. Short answer "If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven." To whom did Christ say this?

  4. Multiple choice What are the three acts of the penitent?

    1. A. Fasting, singing and giving
    2. B. Contrition, confession and satisfaction (the "penance")
    3. C. Reading, writing and praying
    4. D. Kneeling, standing and sitting

  5. True or false In serious cases a priest may reveal what he heard in confession.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Multiple choice Who may receive the Anointing of the Sick?

    1. A. Only someone at the point of death
    2. B. Anyone in danger from sickness or old age, and it may be repeated
    3. C. Only priests
    4. D. Only children

  7. Short answer Name two effects of the Anointing of the Sick.

  8. Multiple choice Which parable shows the merciful father welcoming his son home?

    1. A. The Good Samaritan
    2. B. The prodigal son
    3. C. The sower
    4. D. The talents
