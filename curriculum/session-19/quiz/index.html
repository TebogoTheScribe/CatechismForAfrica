Vocations of Love and Service: Marriage and Holy Orders

Readings for the week

Key question: How does God call people to holiness and service through marriage and ordination?

From the Catechism

CCC 1533–1535, 1536–1538, 1554, 1581–1589, 1601–1605, 1625–1632, 1633–1637, 1638–1642, 1643–1651, 1655–1658, 2387

From the Bible

  • Genesis 2:18–24. "The two shall become one flesh."
  • Mark 10:2–12. "What God has joined together, let no man put asunder."
  • John 2:1–11. Jesus' first sign, at a wedding in Cana.
  • Ephesians 5:21–33. Mutual submission; marriage as an image of Christ and the Church.
  • Hebrews 5:1–6. The high priest, chosen from among men.
