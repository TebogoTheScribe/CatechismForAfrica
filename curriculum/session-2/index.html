God Speaks: Revelation, Scripture and Tradition

Slide 1 of 13

In this session

How has God made himself known, and where do I hear his voice today? In the Creed we profess what God has revealed. God speaks in deeds and words, in Sacred Scripture and Sacred Tradition, and fully in Jesus Christ.

Human reason can know that God exists. God also freely reveals himself. In these last days he has spoken through his Son. Christ is the fullness of Revelation.

Sacred Tradition is the living handing-on of what the apostles received from Christ. Sacred Scripture is the Word of God in writing. The two flow from the same divine wellspring. Tradition, Scripture and the Magisterium work together.

The Catholic Bible has 73 books: 46 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament. Scripture is the Word of God in human language, inspired by God, and the human writers were true authors too.

The slides also set out the Seven Steps of Gospel sharing, ask which Bible story or saying has stayed with you, and close with the Glory Be.
