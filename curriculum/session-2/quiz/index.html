God Speaks: Revelation, Scripture and Tradition

Readings for the week

Key question: How has God made himself known, and where do I hear his voice today?

From the Catechism

CCC 50–53, 74–83, 85–87, 95, 101–108, 120, 131–133, 138

From the Bible

  • Hebrews 1:1–2. God spoke in many ways through the prophets; in these last days he has spoken through his Son.
  • Luke 24:13–35. On the road to Emmaus, the risen Jesus explains the Scriptures and is recognised in the breaking of the bread.
  • 2 Timothy 3:14–17. All Scripture is inspired by God.
  • 2 Thessalonians 2:15. Hold to the traditions you were taught, by word of mouth or by letter.
