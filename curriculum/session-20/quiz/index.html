Quiz

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice In what is the dignity of the human person rooted?

    1. A. Wealth
    2. B. Creation in the image and likeness of God
    3. C. Education
    4. D. Nationality

  2. Short answer Where in the Gospels do we find the Beatitudes?

  3. Multiple choice What are the four cardinal virtues?

    1. A. Faith, hope, charity and prudence
    2. B. Prudence, justice, fortitude and temperance
    3. C. Wisdom, knowledge, piety and fear
    4. D. Patience, kindness, joy and peace

  4. Multiple choice What are the theological virtues?

    1. A. Faith, hope and charity
    2. B. Prudence, justice and fortitude
    3. C. Love, joy and peace
    4. D. Wisdom, understanding and counsel

  5. True or false Conscience never needs to be formed; whatever we feel is right.

  6. Short answer Name the three conditions for a mortal sin.
