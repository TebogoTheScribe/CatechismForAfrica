Chosen by Name: The Rite of Election and the Call to Continuing Conversion

Readings for the week

Key question: What does it mean that God and the Church choose me?

From the Catechism

CCC 540, 1229, 1232, 1247–1249, 1253–1255, 1427–1439, 2043

From the Bible

  • Isaiah 43:1–4. "I have called you by name, you are mine."
  • Joel 2:12–13. "Return to me with all your heart." (Ash Wednesday)
  • Genesis 2:7–9; 3:1–7. Creation and the Fall (Lent 1, Year A).
  • Matthew 4:1–11. Jesus is tempted in the desert (Lent 1, Year A).

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice At the Rite of Election, where are the catechumens' names written?

    1. A. In the parish newsletter
    2. B. In the Book of the Elect
    3. C. On the church door
    4. D. In the baptismal register

  2. True or false Baptised candidates also sign the Book of the Elect.

    Circle one: True / False

  3. True or false Being chosen at the Rite of Election is a prize for good behaviour.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice According to canon law, a sponsor must be at least:

    1. A. Twelve
    2. B. Sixteen
    3. C. Eighteen
    4. D. Twenty-one

  5. True or false A baptised non-Catholic may serve only as a witness of the baptism, together with a Catholic sponsor.

    Circle one: True / False
