Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Short answer With what words do the commandments begin (Exodus 20:2)?

  2. Multiple choice What is "the first act of the virtue of religion"?

    1. A. Fasting
    2. B. Adoration
    3. C. Almsgiving
    4. D. Pilgrimage

  3. True or false The Catechism forbids honouring ancestors.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice What is idolatry?

    1. A. "Divinizing what is not God"
    2. B. Praying before an image of a saint
    3. C. Respecting one's parents
    4. D. Keeping Sunday

  5. Short answer Name two practices that the first commandment forbids.

  6. True or false The Christian veneration of images is contrary to the first commandment.

    Circle one: True / False

  7. Multiple choice What does the second commandment prescribe?

    1. A. Rest on Sunday
    2. B. Respect for the Lord's name
    3. C. Honour for parents
    4. D. Truthfulness

  8. Short answer Give an example of a serious reason that excuses a Catholic from Sunday Mass.

  9. Multiple choice In Acts 19:18–20, what do the new believers in Ephesus do?

    1. A. Burn their books of magic
    2. B. Build a temple
    3. C. Leave the city
