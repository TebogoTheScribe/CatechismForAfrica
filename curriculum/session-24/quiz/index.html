The First Scrutiny: Living Water (John 4)

Back to Session 24 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: What is the thirst in me that only Christ can satisfy?

From the Catechism

CCC 694, 1215, 1237, 1262–1266, 1427–1429, 1673, 2560–2561

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 694
  • CCC 1215
  • CCC 1237
  • CCC 1262–1266
  • CCC 1427–1429
  • CCC 1673
  • CCC 2560–2561

From the Bible

  • Exodus 17:3–7. Water from the rock at Massah and Meribah.
  • Psalm 95. "O that today you would listen to his voice! Harden not your hearts."
  • Romans 5:1–2, 5–8. God's love poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit.
  • John 4:5–42. The Samaritan woman at the well.
