The Second Scrutiny: Light for the Blind (John 9)

Back to Session 25 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: Where am I still blind, and how does Christ give sight?

From the Catechism

CCC 748, 1216, 1243, 1846–1851

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 748
  • CCC 1216
  • CCC 1243
  • CCC 1846–1848
  • CCC 1849–1851

From the Bible

  • 1 Samuel 16:1, 6–7, 10–13. David, the youngest, is anointed.
  • Psalm 23. "The Lord is my shepherd."
  • Ephesians 5:8–14. "Once you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord … Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead, and Christ shall give you light."
  • John 9:1–41. The man born blind.

Quiz

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice According to CCC 748, what is the Church's only light?

    1. A. Her own wisdom
    2. B. Christ's light
    3. C. The light of reason alone
    4. D. The Easter candle

  2. Short answer Quoting St Justin, what does CCC 1216 call the bath of Baptism?

  3. Multiple choice What does the baptismal candle signify?

    1. A. That Christ has enlightened the neophyte
    2. B. That the church needs light
    3. C. That the person is now a priest
    4. D. Nothing in particular

  4. True or false To receive God's mercy, we must admit our faults.

    Circle one: True / False

  5. Short answer In John 9, the man born blind comes to faith step by step. What does he finally say to Jesus?

  6. Multiple choice Which kind of blindness is the worst?

    1. A. Pride
    2. B. Poor eyesight
    3. C. Not knowing the Bible
    4. D. Ignorance of history

  7. Multiple choice Complete 1 Samuel 16:7: "Man looks on the outward appearance, but the LORD looks on ..."
