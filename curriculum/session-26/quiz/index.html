Readings for the week

Key question: What still binds me, and what words will I carry for life?

From the Bible

  • Ezekiel 37:12–14. "I will open your graves … and I will put my Spirit within you."
  • Psalm 130. "Out of the depths I cry to you, O Lord."
  • Romans 8:8–11. The Spirit who raised Jesus will give life to your mortal bodies.
  • John 11:1–45. The raising of Lazarus.
  • Matthew 6:9–13. The Lord's Prayer.
