Readings for the week

Key question: What will happen on the holiest night of the year?

From the Catechism

CCC 560, 610–611, 624, 647, 818, 1168–1169, 1217, 1229, 1233, 1237–1244, 1254, 1271, 1275, 1298, 1300, 1304, 1322

From the Bible

  • Exodus 14:15–15:1. Israel passes through the sea from slavery to freedom (always read at the Vigil).
  • Ezekiel 36:16–28. "I will sprinkle clean water upon you … A new heart I will give you."
  • Romans 6:3–11. "We were buried therefore with him by baptism into death."
  • The Vigil Gospel. Matthew 28:1–10 (Year A), Mark 16:1–7 (Year B) or Luke 24:1–12 (Year C). The sample year 2028 is Year C, so Luke is read: "Why do you seek the living among the dead?"
  • For Holy Week: the Passion read on Palm Sunday (Luke in Year C) and John 18:1–19:42 on Good Friday.
