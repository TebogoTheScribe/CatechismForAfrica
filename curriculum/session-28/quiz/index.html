Mystagogy I: What Happened to Us? "Say Amen to What You Are"

Back to Session 28 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: What did the signs of the Vigil mean, and who am I now?

From the Catechism

CCC 1075, 1243, 1254, 1262, 1265, 1268, 1302–1304, 1389, 1391–1397, 1416, 2181

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

From the Bible

  • John 20:19–31. The risen Jesus breathes the Spirit on the disciples and meets Thomas: "My Lord and my God!" (read every year on the Second Sunday of Easter).
  • Acts 2:42–47. The first Christians "devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers."
  • 1 Peter 2:2–10. "Like newborn babes, long for the pure spiritual milk … you are a chosen race, a royal priesthood."
  • Luke 24:13–35. The disciples on the road to Emmaus know Jesus "in the breaking of the bread".

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice Mystagogy proceeds:

    1. A. From the visible to the invisible, from the sign to the thing signified
    2. B. From the invisible to the visible
    3. C. From books to rules
    4. D. From the end to the beginning

  2. Multiple choice What does the white garment symbolise?

    1. A. That the baptised person has "put on Christ" and has risen with Christ
    2. B. Wealth
    3. C. Purity of clothing
    4. D. Membership of a choir

  3. Short answer What is the principal fruit of receiving the Eucharist in Holy Communion?

  4. True or false Holy Communion is the ordinary way to have mortal sins forgiven.

    Circle one: True / False

  5. True or false Like Baptism, Confirmation is given only once.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Multiple choice The baptised share in Christ's:

    1. A. Priesthood, and his prophetic and royal mission
    2. B. Wealth
    3. C. Political power
    4. D. Earthly kingdom

  7. Short answer Complete CCC 2181: "The Sunday Eucharist is the foundation and confirmation of ..."

  8. Short answer In John 20:19–31, what does Thomas say when he meets the risen Jesus?

  9. Multiple choice At Communion, the minister says "The Body of Christ". How do you answer?

    1. A. Thank you
    2. B. "Amen"
    3. C. Nothing at all
    4. D. Your own name
