  2. Multiple choice How does St Teresa describe contemplative prayer?

    1. A. "A close sharing between friends"
    2. B. A duty
    3. C. A long speech
    4. D. Silence without God

  3. Multiple choice How does the Catechism describe the precepts of the Church?

    1. A. "The indispensable minimum in the spirit of prayer and moral effort"
    2. B. Optional advice
    3. C. Rules for priests only
    4. D. Old customs

  4. Short answer Name two precepts of the Church.

  5. True or false The Church on earth is by her nature missionary.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Multiple choice Who is "the principal agent of the whole of the Church's mission"?
