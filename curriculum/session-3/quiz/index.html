I Believe: Faith as Our Response

Readings for the week

Key question: What is faith, and how is it different from just having an opinion?

From the Catechism

CCC 142–150, 153–165, 166–175, 181

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 142–143
  • CCC 144–149
  • CCC 150

  • CCC 181
  • CCC 1253–1255
  • CCC 2111

From the Bible

  • Genesis 12:1–4. Abraham leaves his homeland at God's word.
  • Luke 1:26–38. Mary's "let it be done to me according to your word."
  • Mark 9:20–24. The father's honest cry: "I believe; help my unbelief!"
  • Hebrews 11:1–12. The great "roll call" of faith.
  • Romans 10:8–17. Faith comes from hearing.

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice Who is called the "father of all who believe"?

    1. A. Moses
    2. B. Abraham
    3. C. David
    4. D. Peter

  2. Multiple choice What is our fitting response when God speaks to us "as his friends"?

    1. A. Fear
    2. B. Faith
    3. C. Silence
    4. D. Argument

  3. True or false Believing destroys our freedom and our intelligence.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice Why is faith certain?

    1. A. Because we feel it strongly
    2. B. Because it rests on God, who cannot lie
    3. C. Because science proves every article of faith
    4. D. Because many people believe it

  5. True or false Real science and faith never contradict each other.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Short answer In Mark 9:24, what does the father of the sick boy cry out to Jesus?

  7. Short answer Faith can be lost. Name two ways we nourish it.

  8. True or false Faith is purely private and has nothing to do with the Church.

    Circle one: True / False

  9. Short answer According to Romans 10:8–17, faith comes from what?
