Readings for the week

Key question: Whose witness will I follow, and how do I keep going?

From the Catechism

CCC 162, 731–736, 828, 1287, 1302–1303, 1830–1832, 2013, 2015, 2030, 2473–2474, 2683–2684

From the Bible

  • Acts 2:1–11. The Spirit comes like wind and fire, and each one hears the Gospel "in his own language".
  • Hebrews 12:1–2. "Since we are surrounded by so great a cloud of witnesses … let us run with perseverance."
  • Revelation 7:9–17. "A great multitude which no man could number, from every nation, from all tribes and peoples and tongues."
  • John 15:12–17. "Greater love has no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends."
