One God, Three Persons: The Holy Trinity

CCC 198–202, 232–237, 238–248, 249–255, 257–260, 261–267

In this session

Key question: If there is only one God, why do Christians speak of Father, Son and Holy Spirit?

CCC references

CCC 198–202, 232–237, 238–248, 249–255, 257–260, 261–267

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 198
  • CCC 199–202
  • CCC 232–255, 257–267
  • CCC 1239–1240
  • CCC 2157

Scripture

  • Deuteronomy 6:4–5. The Shema: the Lord is one.
  • Luke 3:21–22. At Jesus' baptism, the Spirit descends and the Father's voice speaks.
  • Matthew 28:19. Baptise "in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."
  • John 14:16–17, 26. Jesus promises the Holy Spirit.
  • 2 Corinthians 13:14. "The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ and the love of God and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all", a greeting still used at Mass.

Also cited

  • Church Father: St Irenaeus, Against Heresies IV.20.1
