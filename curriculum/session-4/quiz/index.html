One God, Three Persons: The Holy Trinity

Readings for the week: God the Father

Class: "I believe in God the Father", the first Person of the Holy Trinity (CCC 198 and following).

Read slowly, a few paragraphs a day. The Catechism of the Catholic Church (CCC) is free on the Vatican website: https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM.

From the Catechism

CCCTopicWhy it matters
198–202"I believe in one God"The Creed begins with God the Father; with Israel we confess that God is one (CCC 201), "unchangeable" and "entirely simple" (CCC 202).
203–209God reveals his nameGod is "not an anonymous force" (CCC 203); at the burning bush he reveals the name YHWH, "I AM WHO AM" (CCC 206).
210–213"God alone IS"God's name shows his mercy and faithfulness; in him "there is no variation or shadow due to change" (CCC 212).
214–221God is Truth and LoveThe God who reveals himself can be trusted, because he is Truth, and "God's very being is love" (CCC 221).
238–242The Father revealed by the SonJesus shows that God is "Father in an unheard-of sense", eternally Father of his only Son (CCC 240).
50–53How God reveals himselfGod makes himself known "by deeds and words" and gradually, by stages, until his Revelation reaches its fullness in Jesus Christ (CCC 53).
185–197The CreedsWhy we have creeds, why they are called "creeds" and "symbols of faith" (CCC 187), and where the Apostles' and Nicene Creeds come from (CCC 194–195).
14 and 1816Professing the faithThose who belong to Christ "must confess their baptismal faith before men" (CCC 14); faith is to be kept, professed and witnessed to (CCC 1816).
2471–2474Witness and martyrdom"Martyrdom is the supreme witness given to the truth of the faith" (CCC 2473); the Church keeps the acts of the martyrs (CCC 2474).

On the Creed at Mass (not in the Catechism): the General Instruction of the Roman Missal (GIRM) 67–68 and 137 explains that the Creed is sung or said on Sundays and solemnities, by all, standing, after the homily and before the Prayer of the Faithful. https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccdds/documents/rc_con_ccdds_doc_20030317_ordinamento-messale_en.html

From the Bible

PassageWhy it matters
Exodus 3:1–15The burning bush. God reveals his name to Moses (verse 14: "I am who I am", NABRE; the CCC gives it as "I AM WHO AM").
Deuteronomy 6:4–5The Shema: "Hear, O Israel! The LORD is our God, the LORD alone!" The faith in one God that the Creed begins with.
Malachi 3:6 and James 1:17God does not change: "I, the LORD, do not change"; with the Father of lights there is "no alteration or shadow caused by change".
Hebrews 1:1–2God spoke "in partial and various ways" through the prophets and now through his Son: how God reveals himself step by step.
Matthew 11:25–27"No one knows the Father except the Son and anyone to whom the Son wishes to reveal him."
Romans 10:9–10"One believes with the heart … and one confesses with the mouth": faith is meant to be spoken.
Matthew 10:32–33"Everyone who acknowledges me before others I will acknowledge before my heavenly Father."
Acts 4:18–20 and 1 Timothy 6:12–13Peter and John, ordered to be silent: "It is impossible for us not to speak"; Jesus gave "the noble confession" under Pontius Pilate.

Scripture quotations are from the New American Bible, Revised Edition (NABRE), checked on bible.usccb.org. Wording differs slightly in other Catholic translations such as the RSV-2CE and the Jerusalem Bible.

Quiz: God the Father

Name:

Answer all ten questions. You may use your Bible.

  1. Multiple choice At the burning bush, how does God reveal his name to Moses (Exodus 3:14)?

    1. A. The Almighty
    2. B. I AM WHO AM
    3. C. The Most High
    4. D. The Lord of Hosts

  2. Multiple choice Out of respect for God's holiness, what did the people of Israel say instead of pronouncing the revealed name (YHWH) when reading Scripture?

    1. A. Abba
    2. B. LORD (Adonai in Hebrew, Kyrios in Greek)
    3. C. Messiah
    4. D. Emmanuel

  3. True or false God revealed himself all at once, without preparing his people.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice Which verse says that with the Father "there is no alteration or shadow caused by change"?

    1. A. Genesis 1:1
    2. B. John 3:16
    3. C. James 1:17
    4. D. Luke 2:11

  5. True or false When the Church says God's nature is "entirely simple", it means God is not made up of parts: he is not a mixture of pieces or qualities, but wholly himself.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Short answer The word "creed" comes from the first word of the Creed in Latin. What is that word, and what does it mean?

  7. Multiple choice The Nicene Creed we say at Sunday Mass comes from the first two ecumenical councils. When were they held?

    1. A. 70 and 110
    2. B. 325 and 381
    3. C. 451 and 553
    4. D. 1545 and 1563

  8. Short answer St Perpetua, a young mother martyred in North Africa in the early third century, was told by the Roman official to "offer sacrifice for the well-being of the emperors". When he asked, "Are you a Christian?", what did she answer? In one sentence, why does her answer matter for us when we say the Creed?

  9. Multiple choice At Sunday Mass, when do we profess the Creed?

    1. A. At the very beginning of Mass
    2. B. Straight after the Gospel, before the homily
    3. C. After the homily, before the Prayer of the Faithful
    4. D. After Holy Communion

  10. True or false The Creed is said at every weekday Mass.

    Circle one: True / False

Your catechist has the answers.
