Session 5 · Readings and quiz

Created Good, Wounded by Sin: Creation, Human Dignity and the Fall

Back to Session 5 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: If God made everything good, why is the world, and my own heart, so broken?

From the Catechism

CCC 279, 290–295, 299, 355–361, 369–373, 385–390, 396–409, 410–412

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 279, 290–295, 299
  • CCC 355–361, 369–373
  • CCC 385–390, 396–412
  • CCC 1237
  • CCC 1263

From the Bible

  • Genesis 1:26–31. Male and female he created them; God saw that it was very good.
  • Genesis 2:7, 15. Formed from the dust; placed in the garden "to till it and keep it".
  • Genesis 3:1–15. The temptation, the fall, and the promise.
  • Psalm 8. "What is man that you are mindful of him?"
  • Romans 5:12–19. As through one man sin came, so through one man, Jesus, grace abounds.
  • Romans 7:19 (quoted in the video) and Genesis 3:9 ("Where are you?").

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice Why did God create the world?

    1. A. Because he needed it
    2. B. Freely, by wisdom and love
    3. C. By accident
    4. D. To keep himself busy

  2. Short answer The Catechism quotes St Irenaeus: the glory of God is the human person ... Complete the phrase.

  3. True or false Every human being has the dignity of a person, not a thing.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice How are man and woman created?

    1. A. Unequal in dignity
    2. B. In perfect equality as persons, and for each other
    3. C. As rivals
    4. D. Only for work

  5. True or false Original sin is a personal fault that each of us committed.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Multiple choice What does Baptism do about original sin?

    1. A. Nothing
    2. B. It erases original sin, but the inclination to sin (concupiscence) remains as a struggle
    3. C. It removes every temptation for ever
    4. D. It only hides it

  7. Short answer Genesis 3:15 is called "the first Gospel". What is its Latin name, and what does it promise?

  8. Multiple choice In Genesis 2:15, why does God place the man in the garden?

    1. A. To rule it as a tyrant
    2. B. "To till it and keep it"
    3. C. To rest for ever
    4. D. To name the stars

  9. Short answer Romans 5:12–19 says that sin came through one man. Through whom does grace abound?

Your catechist has the answers.
