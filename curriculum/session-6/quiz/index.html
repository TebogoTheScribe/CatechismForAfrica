Readings for the week

Key question: Jesus asks, "Who do you say that I am?" What is my answer?

From the Catechism

CCC 422–429, 456–463, 464–469, 470–478, 480–483

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 422–429
  • CCC 456–478, 480–483
  • CCC 2667–2668

From the Bible

  • John 1:1–14. "The Word became flesh and dwelt among us."
  • Philippians 2:5–11. He emptied himself, taking the form of a servant.
  • Mark 8:27–30. "But who do you say that I am?" (This was the Gospel at the Angelus on 13 September 2015, the day of Benedict Daswa's beatification.)
  • Luke 2:1–20. Born in poverty, announced to shepherds.

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Short answer CCC 422 quotes Galatians 4:4: "When the time had fully come, God sent forth his ..." Complete the verse.

  2. Short answer Give two reasons why the Word became flesh.

  3. True or false Jesus is part God and part man, a mixture of the two.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice Which early Councils defended the truth about Christ?

    1. A. Nicaea (325), Ephesus (431) and Chalcedon (451)
    2. B. Trent, Vatican I and Vatican II
    3. C. Only the Council of Jerusalem
    4. D. None

  5. True or false Christ's humanity has a real human soul, intellect and will.

    Circle one: True / False

  6. Multiple choice What is the distinctive sign of Christian faith?

    1. A. Belief in reincarnation
    2. B. Belief in the true Incarnation of the Son of God
    3. C. Belief in many gods
    4. D. Belief in fate

  7. Short answer In Mark 8:27–30, what question does Jesus put to his disciples?

  8. Multiple choice Why is Jesus the one mediator between God and humanity?

    1. A. Because he was a great prophet
    2. B. Because he is God and man in one Person
    3. C. Because he was a good teacher
    4. D. Because he was a king

  9. Short answer According to Philippians 2:5–11, what did Christ take when he emptied himself?

Your catechist has the answers.
