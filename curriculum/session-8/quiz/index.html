Readings for the week

Key question: Why did Jesus have to die, and what changed when he rose?

From the Catechism

CCC 571–573, 599–605, 606–618, 631–637, 638–655, 656–658

Read these paragraphs in the Catechism on vatican.va:

  • CCC 571–573
  • CCC 597–618
  • CCC 631
  • CCC 632–637
  • CCC 638–658
  • CCC 1227
  • CCC 1367

From the Bible

  • Isaiah 52:13–53:12. The Suffering Servant: "by his stripes we are healed."
  • Mark 15:1–39. The Passion; the centurion's confession.
  • 1 Corinthians 15:3–8, 12–20. The earliest summary of the Gospel, and the witnesses.
  • Luke 24:1–12. The empty tomb.

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Multiple choice What stands at the centre of the Good News?

    1. A. The miracles of Jesus
    2. B. The Paschal Mystery of Christ's Cross and Resurrection
    3. C. The Sermon on the Mount
    4. D. The birth of Jesus

  2. True or false Jesus' violent death was the result of chance.

    Circle one: True / False

  3. True or false The Church blames "the Jews" collectively for the death of Jesus.

    Circle one: True / False

  4. Multiple choice For whom did Christ die?

    1. A. Only for good people
    2. B. For all without exception
    3. C. Only for Christians
    4. D. Only for the apostles

  5. Short answer Jesus says, "No one takes [my life] from me, but I lay it down of my own accord" (John 10:18). What does this show about his death?

  6. Multiple choice Why did Christ descend to the realm of the dead?

    1. A. To judge the wicked
    2. B. To open heaven's gates for the just who had gone before him
    3. C. To rest
    4. D. To visit the angels

  7. True or false The Resurrection is a historical event witnessed by the disciples, and also a mystery that goes beyond history.

    Circle one: True / False

  8. Short answer Which letter of St Paul contains the earliest summary of the Gospel and lists the witnesses of the risen Christ?

  9. Multiple choice Isaiah 52:13–53:12 speaks of:

    1. A. The Suffering Servant
    2. B. The burning bush
    3. C. Noah's ark
    4. D. The creation of the world

Your catechist has the answers.
