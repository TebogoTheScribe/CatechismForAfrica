The Holy Spirit and the Church

Back to Session 9 · All sessions

Readings for the week

Key question: What is the Church, and how does the Holy Spirit give her life?

From the Catechism

CCC 683–690, 731–741, 748–757, 775–776, 787–796, 811–822, 830–838, 846–848, 857–865, 880–896

From the Bible

  • Acts 2:1–42. Pentecost; Peter's preaching; three thousand baptised; "they devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and fellowship, to the breaking of bread and the prayers."
  • 1 Corinthians 12:12–27. One body, many members.
  • Matthew 16:13–19. "You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church."
  • Ephesians 4:1–6. One Lord, one faith, one baptism.

Quiz

Name:

Answer all nine questions. You may use your Bible and the Catechism.

  1. Short answer No one can say "Jesus is Lord" except by whom?

  2. Multiple choice What happened at Pentecost?

    1. A. The Spirit was poured out, the Trinity was fully revealed and the Church was manifested to the world
    2. B. Jesus was born
    3. C. The Ten Commandments were given
    4. D. Jesus was baptised

  3. Multiple choice The Church is "like a sacrament". She is a sign and instrument of what?

    1. A. Political power
    2. B. Communion with God and the unity of the human race
    3. C. Wealth
    4. D. One nation only

  4. True or false Christ is the Head of the Church, and we are the members of his Body.

    Circle one: True / False

  5. Short answer Name the four marks of the Church professed in the Creed.

  6. True or false Those born into separated Christian communities are to blame for the divisions.

    Circle one: True / False

  7. Multiple choice What does "Outside the Church there is no salvation" mean, said positively?

    1. A. Only Catholics can ever be saved
    2. B. All salvation comes from Christ the Head through the Church, his Body; it does not condemn those who, through no fault of their own, do not know Christ or the Church but seek God sincerely
    3. C. Salvation does not matter
    4. D. Good works alone save us

  8. Multiple choice Who continue to teach and guide the Church as successors of the apostles?

    1. A. The bishops, with Peter's successor, the Pope
    2. B. Kings and presidents
    3. C. Each family alone
    4. D. Theologians alone

  9. Short answer According to Acts 2:42, to what four things did the first Christians devote themselves?

Your catechist has the answers.
