Advent: Why Do We Wait?

Christmas is coming, so why does the Church make us wait?

AI-assisted explainer, reviewed by Tebogo Mokwena

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Advent is a season of waiting for Christ, who has come and will come again.
