The Advent Wreath: Purple, Rose and Light

What do the candles and the purple mean?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

The Advent wreath is a much-loved custom of light, colour and fire that marks the weeks until Christmas.

  • The liturgy speaks through signs: light, colour, fire (CCC 1145–1146, 1189).
  • Violet marks a season of hope, prayer and turning back to God.
  • The rose candle belongs to the Third Sunday (Gaudete, 13 December): "Rejoice… the Lord is near."
  • Light growing week by week points to Christ, the Light of the world.
  • The wreath is a much-loved home and parish custom, not a required part of the liturgy.

References
