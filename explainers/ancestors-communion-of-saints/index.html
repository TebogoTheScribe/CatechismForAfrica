Explainer 05 · 1 min 12 s

Ancestors and the Communion of Saints

Can a Catholic honour the ancestors?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Catholics honour the ancestors and the saints in Christ; we do not worship them or seek power from the dead.

  • We remain one family in Christ: on earth, being purified, and in glory (CCC 954).
  • Praying for our dead is a holy and ancient practice (CCC 958, 1032; 2 Maccabees 12:46).
  • The saints intercede for us; we honour them, we do not worship them (CCC 956).
  • Divination, consulting the dead and seeking power through charms are rejected (CCC 2116–2117).
  • Respect for parents and elders, and remembering them in prayer and at Mass, fits the Gospel.
