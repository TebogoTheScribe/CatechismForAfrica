Explainer 16 · 1 min 12 s

Anointing of the Sick

Is Anointing only for the dying?

AI-assisted explainer, reviewed by Tebogo Mokwena

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Anointing of the Sick is not only for the dying; it is for those in danger from illness or old age.

  • It is not reserved for the last moment. It is for those who begin to be in danger from illness or old age (CCC 1514).
  • The letter of James tells the sick to call for the elders, who pray and anoint with oil (James 5:14–15; CCC 1511).
  • It unites the sick person to the passion of Christ, gives strength, may restore health, and prepares us for the final journey (CCC 1520–1523, 1532).
