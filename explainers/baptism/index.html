Explainer 12 · 1 min 12 s

Baptism

Why is Baptism the beginning?

AI-assisted explainer, reviewed by Tebogo Mokwena

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Baptism is the gateway to life in the Spirit: we are born of water and the Spirit and raised with Christ.

  • Baptism is the gateway to life in the Spirit and the door to the other sacraments (CCC 1213).
  • We are born of water and the Spirit (John 3:5), buried with Christ and raised with him (Romans 6:3–4).
  • The risen Lord sends the Church to baptise all nations (Matthew 28:19).
  • Baptism forgives sin, makes us children of God, and joins us to the Body of Christ (CCC 1262–1270).
