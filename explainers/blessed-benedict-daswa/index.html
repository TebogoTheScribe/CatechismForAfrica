Saints of Africa: Blessed Benedict Daswa

Who was South Africa's first beatified martyr?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Blessed Benedict Daswa, a teacher from Mbahe, Limpopo, was killed in 1990 for refusing to take part in witchcraft, and was beatified in 2015.

  • A Venda teacher, catechist, husband and father from Mbahe, Limpopo.
  • He refused to contribute to hiring a witch-finder, because it went against his faith.
  • He was killed on 2 February 1990, praying as he died.
  • Beatified at Tshitanini on 13 September 2015; feast day 1 February.
  • Every Christian is called to holiness; martyrdom is the supreme witness (CCC 2473, 2013).
