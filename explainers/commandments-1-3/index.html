Love God Above All: Commandments 1–3

What does it mean to have no other gods?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

The first three commandments teach us how to love God first: worship him alone, honour his Name, and keep his Day holy.

  • Jesus sums up the Law: love God with all your heart, and love your neighbour as yourself (Matthew 22:37–39; CCC 2055).
  • The first commandment is to worship God alone and to serve him (CCC 2084). Idolatry is putting a creature in the place of God (CCC 2113).
  • Divination, consulting the dead and wearing charms for power or protection are rejected (CCC 2116–2117; Deuteronomy 18:10–12).
  • The second commandment: the Lord’s Name is holy; we speak it with reverence (CCC 2142).
  • The third commandment: keep holy the Lord’s Day. For Christians, Sunday fulfils the Sabbath, and the faithful are bound to take part in the Mass (CCC 2174–2175, 2180; Exodus 20:8).
