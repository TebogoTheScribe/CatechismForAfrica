Explainer 28 · 1 min 8 s

Love Your Neighbour: Commandments 4–10

How does love of God become justice and mercy for my neighbour?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

Commandments 4 to 10 protect family, life, marriage, property, truth and the heart. Love of God becomes justice and mercy for the person in front of you.

  • The second tablet begins: after God, honour your father and mother (CCC 2196–2198; Exodus 20:12).
  • The commandments that follow guard life, marriage, goods, speech and the heart (CCC 2258, 2331, 2401, 2464, 2514).
  • “Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu”: a person is a person through other persons. This says something the Church also teaches about the common good and solidarity (CCC 1905, 1939). Christ takes it further: the neighbour includes the stranger and even the enemy (Luke 10:25–37).
  • The works of mercy — feeding, sheltering, visiting, burying — are a chief witness to this love (CCC 2447; Matthew 25:31–46).
