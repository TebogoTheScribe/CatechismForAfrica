Explainer 22 · 1 min 12 s

Created Good, Wounded by Sin

If God made everything good, why is the world, and my own heart, so broken?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

God made us good, in his image. Sin is real and it wounded us; it did not have the last word.

  • The human person is created in the image of God, male and female; God saw that it was very good (Genesis 1:26–31; CCC 355–356).
  • Only the light of Revelation makes the reality of sin clear; it is not merely weakness or a social flaw (CCC 387).
  • At the beginning, man freely refused God (CCC 396–397). Original sin is a truth of faith (CCC 388–389).
  • We inherit a wounded nature, not a destroyed one (CCC 404–405).
  • After the fall, God promised a Saviour (Genesis 3:15; CCC 410–411). Christ is the new Adam (Romans 5:12–19).
