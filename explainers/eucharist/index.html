Explainer 14 · 1 min 12 s

The Eucharist

Why is the Eucharist the heart of the Church?

AI-assisted explainer, reviewed by Tebogo Mokwena

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

The Eucharist is the source and summit of the Christian life, in which the whole Christ is truly present.

  • The Eucharist is the source and summit of the Christian life (CCC 1324).
  • “This is my body… Do this in memory of me.” (Luke 22:19–20; 1 Corinthians 11:23–26).
  • “I am the living bread that came down from heaven.” (John 6:51).
  • The whole Christ is truly, really and substantially present: body and blood, soul and divinity (CCC 1374).
