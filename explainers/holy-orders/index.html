Explainer 17 · 1 min 12 s

Holy Orders

Why does the Church have bishops, priests and deacons?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

Holy Orders is the sacrament of apostolic ministry, with three degrees: bishop, priest and deacon.

  • Holy Orders is the sacrament of apostolic ministry (CCC 1536).
  • There are three degrees: bishop, priest and deacon (CCC 1554).
  • The ordained are marked with an indelible character and serve in the person of Christ the Head, for the sake of the whole Church (CCC 1581–1583; John 20:21).
