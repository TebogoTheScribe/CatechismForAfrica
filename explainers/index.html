Explainers

Short, free videos on questions people ask, the faith, the last things, the commandments, the seven sacraments, Advent, and a saint of Africa. Each one is built on the Catechism and points back to the parish curriculum.

Mary Immaculate: Conceived Without Sin

Christ gave the Church the power to forgive sins, so in Confession we hear the words of absolution spoken aloud.

The Last Things

Death, judgement, heaven and hell, and why we pray for the dead.

Last Things

Explainer 25 · 1 min 12 s

The Four Last Things

Death, judgement, heaven and hell are not scare-stories. They are the truth about our destiny, and our hope is Christ.
Last Things

Explainer 26 · 1 min 12 s

Purgatory is not a second chance after a final no. It is God’s mercy, finishing the purification of those who die in his friendship.
