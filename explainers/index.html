Eighteen short films · about 60–90 seconds

Explainers

Short, free films on questions people ask, the seven sacraments, Advent, and a saint of Africa. Each one is built on the Catechism and points back to the parish curriculum.

Advent & Christmas

Waiting, the wreath, Mary Immaculate, and why the Word became flesh.

Advent

Explainer 01 · 1 min 13 s

Advent: Why Do We Wait?

Advent is a season of waiting for Christ, who has come and will come again.
Advent

Explainer 02 · 1 min 9 s

The Advent Wreath: Purple, Rose and Light

The Advent wreath is a much-loved custom of light, colour and fire that marks the weeks until Christmas.
Advent

Explainer 03 · 1 min 11 s

Mary Immaculate: Conceived Without Sin

Mary was preserved from original sin from the first moment of her conception, by the merits of her Son.
Advent

Explainer 06 · 1 min 12 s

Why Did God Become Man?

The Word became flesh to save us, to show us God’s love, to be our model, and to share his divine life.

Questions people ask

Confession, ancestors, the shape of the Mass, Scripture and Tradition, and lobola.

Question

Explainer 04 · 1 min 12 s

Why Confess to a Priest?

Christ gave the Church the power to forgive sins, so in Confession we hear the words of absolution spoken aloud.
Question

Explainer 05 · 1 min 12 s

Ancestors and the Communion of Saints

Catholics honour the ancestors and the saints in Christ; we do not worship them or seek power from the dead.
Question

Explainer 07 · 1 min 12 s

Walk Through the Mass: The Shape of the Mass

The Mass has the same shape as Emmaus and the first Church: Scripture explained, then bread broken.
Question

Explainer 08 · 1 min 12 s

Isn’t the Bible Enough?

Scripture and Tradition flow from the same well; the Church recognised the canon and reads the Bible as a family.
Question

Explainer 09 · 1 min 12 s

Lobola and Catholic Marriage

Lobola can honour families, but a Catholic marriage is made by free consent, according to the Church’s form.
