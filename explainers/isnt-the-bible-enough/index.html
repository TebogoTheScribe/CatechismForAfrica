Explainer 08 · 1 min 12 s

Isn’t the Bible Enough?

Why do Catholics need Tradition as well as the Bible?

AI-assisted explainer, reviewed by Tebogo Mokwena

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Scripture and Tradition flow from the same well; the Church recognised the canon and reads the Bible as a family.

  • The Gospel was handed on in two ways: by preaching and by writing (CCC 75–76).
  • Scripture and Tradition flow from the same source and belong together (CCC 80–82, 97).
  • It was through Tradition that the Church recognised which books belong in the Bible (CCC 120).
  • The Magisterium serves the Word of God; it is not above it (CCC 95).
