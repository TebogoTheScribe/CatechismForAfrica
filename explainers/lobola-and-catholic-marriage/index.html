Explainer 09 · 1 min 12 s

Lobola and Catholic Marriage

If we have done lobola, are we married in the eyes of the Church?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Lobola can honour families, but a Catholic marriage is made by free consent, according to the Church’s form.

  • Marriage is God's own plan from the beginning (CCC 1601).
  • Consent freely given by a man and a woman makes the marriage (CCC 1625–1626).
  • Catholics normally marry according to the Church's form, so speak to the parish priest early (CCC 1631).
  • Customs that honour and unite families can be welcomed, as long as they serve the dignity of the couple (CCC 1204–1206).
  • Conjugal love is total, faithful and open to life (CCC 1643).
