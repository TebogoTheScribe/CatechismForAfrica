Explainer 21 · 1 min 12 s

One God, Three Persons

If there is only one God, why do Christians speak of Father, Son and Holy Spirit?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

We do not worship three gods. The one God has revealed himself as Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

  • “Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord” (Deuteronomy 6:4; CCC 201–202).
  • We are baptised in the name — not the names — of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit (Matthew 28:19; CCC 232–233).
  • The Trinity is One: we do not confess three gods, but one God in three persons (CCC 253).
  • The persons are really distinct: the Father is not the Son, nor is the Spirit the Father or the Son (CCC 254).
  • This is a mystery of faith in the strict sense, known because God has revealed it (CCC 237).
