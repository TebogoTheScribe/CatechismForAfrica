Explainer 25 · 1 min 12 s

The Four Last Things

What happens when we die, and what do we hope for?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

Death, judgement, heaven and hell are not scare-stories. They are the truth about our destiny, and our hope is Christ.

  • Death is the end of earthly life; Christ has transformed it (CCC 1005–1010). We live only once: there is no reincarnation (CCC 1013; Hebrews 9:27).
  • At death each person is judged in the light of Christ (CCC 1021–1022).
  • Heaven is perfect life with the Holy Trinity, the fulfilment of our deepest longings (CCC 1023–1024).
  • Hell is the state of definitive self-exclusion from God (CCC 1033). God predestines no one to hell (CCC 1037). The teaching is a call to conversion (CCC 1036).
  • “I am the Resurrection and the life” (John 11:25–26).
