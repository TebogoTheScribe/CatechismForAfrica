Explainer 24 · 1 min 12 s

The Holy Spirit and the Church

What is the Church, and how does the Holy Spirit give her life?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

The Church is not a club we invented. She is the Body of Christ, and the Holy Spirit is her life.

  • At Pentecost the Spirit is poured out on the Church (Acts 2:1–4; CCC 731–732).
  • The Spirit builds, animates and sanctifies the Church; the Church’s mission is the mission of Christ and the Spirit (CCC 737–739).
  • The Church is the Body of Christ: “You are the body of Christ and individually members of it” (1 Corinthians 12:27; CCC 787–789).
  • We profess the Church to be one, holy, catholic and apostolic (CCC 811).
