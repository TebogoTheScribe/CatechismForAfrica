Explainer 19 · 1 min 12 s

The Restless Heart: Why Did God Make Us?

Why does my heart long for something more?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

The ache for something more is not a fault in us. God made us for himself, and he is already calling.

  • God, infinitely perfect, freely created us to share his own blessed life (CCC 1).
  • The desire for God is written in the human heart; God never ceases to draw us to himself (CCC 27).
  • We can forget God, but he never ceases to call; St Augustine: “You have made us for yourself, and our heart is restless until it rests in you” (CCC 30).
  • Reason can know that God exists; Revelation tells us who he is (CCC 35).
