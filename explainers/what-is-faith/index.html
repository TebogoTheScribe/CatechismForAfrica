Explainer 20 · 1 min 12 s

What Is Faith?

What is faith, and how is it different from just having an opinion?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait video.

Key points

Faith is not a private hunch. It is trust in God and a free yes to the truth he reveals, received in the Church.

  • God addresses us as friends; the right reply is faith (CCC 142–143).
  • Faith is personal adherence to God and, inseparably, assent to the whole truth he has revealed (CCC 150).
  • Faith is a grace, and also a free human act; no one is to be forced (CCC 153, 160).
  • No one believes alone. We say both “I believe” and “We believe” (CCC 166–167).
