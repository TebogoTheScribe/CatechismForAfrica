Explainer 04 · 1 min 12 s

Why Confess to a Priest?

Why not just tell God I am sorry?

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

Christ gave the Church the power to forgive sins, so in Confession we hear the words of absolution spoken aloud.

  • Sin hurts our friendship with God and also wounds the Church (CCC 1440).
  • Jesus gave the apostles power to forgive sins; bishops and priests continue this ministry (CCC 1461).
  • We hear the words of forgiveness spoken aloud: "I absolve you".
  • The seal of confession is absolute: a priest may never reveal what he hears (CCC 1467).
