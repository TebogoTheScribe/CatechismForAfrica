Why Did God Become Man?

Why did God become a baby at Christmas?

AI-assisted explainer, reviewed by Tebogo Mokwena

Desktop: widescreen 16:9. Switch to Mobile for the portrait film.

Key points

The Word became flesh to save us, to show us God’s love, to be our model, and to share his divine life.

  • To save us by reconciling us with God (CCC 457).
  • So that we might know God's love (CCC 458).
  • To be our model of holiness (CCC 459).
  • To make us sharers in the divine life (CCC 460).
