About Catechism for Africa

Traditional Catholic Formation for Africa

Tebogo Mokwena

RCIA Coordinator in the Vaal Diocese since 2024

Mission

To give under-resourced South African and African parishes access to faithful catechesis rooted in the Catechism of the Catholic Church, Sacred Scripture, the Magisterium and Sacred Tradition.

A personal apostolate. Not an official diocesan or parish resource.

Who it is for

Catechumens and candidates first. RCIA coordinators and catechists second. Lifelong Catholics who want to refresh their faith third.

What is here now

Six original whiteboard lessons are on the Catechism for Africa YouTube channel and on the Lessons page. Explainers and apologetics are separate pages, ready for material to be added. Prices are in South African rand. Payments are not open yet. PayFast is planned.

For a parish or diocese that wants the Parish / Coordinator licence: R299 a month or R2,499 a year per parish, for unlimited team use in that parish’s RCIA programme.
